# 🪢 ThirdKnot

### _A Cord of Three Strands is Not Quickly Broken._

**ThirdKnot** is not just another dating app; it is a spiritual ecosystem designed to unite singles with God at the center. Inspired by **Ecclesiastes 4:12**, our mission is to facilitate relationships where the couple and God form an unbreakable bond.

## ✝️ The Concept

In a world of superficial swipes, **ThirdKnot** brings depth. We believe that the strongest relationships are triangles: **You, Your Partner, and God**.
We are reimagining the Christian dating experience by merging **Biblical principles** with **state-of-the-art AI** and **premium, modern aesthetics**. This is not your grandma's Christian dating site—this is a vibrant, immersive platform for the modern believer.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🏛️ The Sunday Seal (Church Verification)

_Accountability meets Community._

-   **Geo-Verified Gathering**: deeply integrated location services verify that users are physically attending their listed church on Sundays.
-   **Church Badges**: Display your home church proudly.
-   **"Gather" Mode**: Sundays unlock special matching algorithms to connect you with people who value worship as much as you do.

### 🤖 The Divine Matchmaker (AI Agent)

_Wisdom in the Cloud._

-   **Faith-Based Compatibility**: Our AI doesn't just match hobbies; it analyzes denomination, spiritual maturity, and life goals to suggest the "Third Knot" connections.
-   **Conversation Starters**: The AI provides icebreakers rooted in scripture and faith-based topics to skip the small talk.
-   **Red Flag/Green Flag Analysis**: Intelligent insights to help you discern if a match aligns with your walk with Christ.

### 🔥 Experience & Design

-   **Premium Aesthetics**: Glassmorphism, subtle gradients, and fluid micro-animations. No beige or boring interfaces.
-   **Vibrant & Alive**: A dynamic interface that feels responsive and exciting to use.
-   **God-Centered UI**: Visual reminders of the "Third Knot" concept woven throughout the user journey.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack (The "Trinity" Stack)

-   **Mobile App**: React Native (via Expo)
    -   _Why?_ Best-in-class for cross-platform (iOS & Android) while sharing logic with the web. Allows for Over-the-Air updates and rapid location-feature development.
-   **Web Portal**: Next.js 14
    -   _Why?_ For the landing page, church administration portal, and SEO.
-   **Shared Core**: TypeScript
    -   _Why?_ Share your types, AI logic, and API calls between Web and Mobile.
-   **Styling**: NativeWind (Tailwind specifically for React Native)
-   **Backend**: Supabase (Postgres + Auth + Realtim)
-   **AI**: OpenAI / Gemini API Integration
-   **Geolocation**: Expo Location + Google Maps API
